short.

Paste a long URL.
Walk away with a short one.

No account. No tracking pitch. One box, built to stay up when a link gets popular.

1. Paste

Any https (or http) address. We store it and give you an id.

2. Share

The short link is this site plus /expand/ and that id.

3. Open

Anyone who hits it is sent to the original URL. That’s the whole product.

API: POST /shorten with {"original_url":"https://…"} → GET /expand/{id}
